CS5487: Machine Learning: Principles and Practice

This course introduces fundamental machine learning principles with an emphasis on mathematical derivation, algorithm design, implementation, and evaluation. Topics include statistical and Bayesian learning, clustering, dimensionality reduction, regression, discriminative classifiers, and kernel methods, with selected connections to modern machine learning.
