Research

Efficient, explainable, and structure-aware machine learning for scientific sensing and large language models.

Across computer vision and signal processing, I study limited-supervision and geometry-aware methods for geoscience and healthcare. In large language models, I focus on compression, efficient inference, uncertainty, and interpretability.
