01 / NeurIPS’26, Poster

Extend ECG models beyond ten seconds

Kangning Cui · Co-author

Key result Experiments across ECG tasks and backbones improve on sliding-window and pooling baselines without retraining the backbone.

Problem
An ECG model trained on 10-second clips cannot directly combine information across a longer, variable-length recording.
Method
Keep the pretrained backbone frozen and add a lightweight module for long-sequence processing and temporal relationships.

02 / MICCAI’25, Oral

Find cardiac phases without manual labels

Kangning Cui · Co-author

Key result On EchoNet-Dynamic, mean absolute error is 3 frames for end-diastole and 2 for end-systole. The study also evaluates fetal echocardiography.

Problem
Cardiac phase detection usually needs manually marked frames or segmentation annotations.
Method
Learn motion trajectories through video reconstruction, then identify end-diastole and end-systole in that representation.
